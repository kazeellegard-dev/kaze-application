FEATURES

Key features

Tap and have fun

Tap big colors and illustrations to play.

Three screens

Switch between the “Colors”, “Cars” and “Cats” screens with the tabs at the bottom.

Simple to use

With few buttons and little text, even small children can play by instinct.

For iPhone and iPad

Pictures are shown large to fit the screen.

HOW TO USE

How to use

The basic flow.

  1. Open the app

    The “Colors” screen appears when you launch it.

  2. Tap

    Tap your favorite colors and pictures to play.

  3. Switch screens

    Use the tabs at the bottom to switch to the “Cars” and “Cats” screens.
