How personal information is handled in our apps

KAZE Application does not obtain or store personal information such as your name, email address or location through our apps. However, in apps that use an ad delivery service, the ad provider may obtain information as described below.

About ad delivery

Apps that show ads use “Google AdMob”, an ad delivery service provided by Google LLC.

AdMob may obtain the following kinds of information about your device and ads, for purposes such as delivering ads, measuring their performance and preventing fraud.

  • IP address (used to estimate the approximate location of your device)
  • Device identifiers
  • Information about ads and how you use the app, such as interactions with ads
  • Diagnostic information such as crash logs and performance data

This information is handled in accordance with Google’s privacy policy. For details, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. KAZE Application does not identify individuals from this information.

Our apps do not ask for permission to track you through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency, and do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).

You can manage ad personalization in Google Ads Settings.

About inquiries

With the contact form on this site, you can send an inquiry using your own email app. KAZE Application uses the name, email address and message you send in order to respond to your inquiry, and does not use them for any other purpose.
